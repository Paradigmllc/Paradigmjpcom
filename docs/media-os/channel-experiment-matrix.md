# 12-channel experiment matrix

## 2026 operating thesis

- Creator advertising is still expanding, but buyers increasingly demand measurable outcomes and clearer creator selection. IAB projects U.S. creator ad spend at $44B in 2026.
- YouTube explicitly treats repetitive, mass-produced, minimally varied output as inauthentic and ineligible for monetization. Automation must reduce production cost while preserving original reporting, structure, visual direction, and commentary per episode.
- High advertiser-intent subject matter is a useful portfolio prior, not a guarantee of RPM. Selection is driven by observed revenue, retention, repeat viewing, sponsor fit, production cost, and risk after publication.

Sources: [IAB Creator Economy report](https://www.iab.com/insights/2025-creator-economy-ad-spend-strategy-report/), [YouTube channel monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en-AU), [YouTube monetizable-content guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2490020?hl=en-EN).

## Staged test

### Wave A — breadth

- Produce two 10–20 minute originals per lane: 24 long-form videos total.
- Reuse infrastructure, design tokens, and render components, but not identical story arcs, narration, scene order, or thumbnails.
- Each long-form becomes the parent of one optional 3–6 minute YouTube summary, two to four Shorts/TikTok/Reels cuts, and one teaser. Derivatives do not count as independent channel experiments.
- Platform cuts receive different hooks, caption layouts, descriptions, and calls to action; identical cross-posts are only a temporary technical test.
- Enforce a per-video cost ceiling and record human minutes, model spend, GPU spend, render time, and revision count.

### Wave B — evidence

- Advance at most six lanes.
- Produce four additional long-form videos per surviving lane.
- Introduce one monetization test per lane: sponsor outreach, qualified affiliate, newsletter capture, or research-product waitlist.

### Scale / iterate / stop

Do not rank a video until it has at least 5,000 eligible impressions or 30 days of exposure. Advance lanes that pass policy and factual QA and either reach the portfolio's top quartile composite score or meet all of these starting thresholds:

- impression click-through rate at least 5%;
- 30-second retention at least 65%;
- average percentage viewed at least 40%;
- positive returning-viewer trend across consecutive releases;
- contribution margin positive after research, human review, GPU, voice, and render costs.

Iterate packaging when retention is sound but click-through is weak. Iterate the opening and scene rhythm when click-through is sound but early retention is weak. Stop a lane after Wave B when it remains in the bottom quartile without a clear packaging-only diagnosis, or immediately for repeated rights, factual, or monetization-policy defects.

## Monetization sequence

1. AdSense and YouTube Premium revenue establish the baseline.
2. Add category-safe sponsorships only after audience fit is visible.
3. Add qualified affiliates with explicit disclosures.
4. Build bilingual newsletters and source manifests as owned audience assets.
5. License explainers or sell research/data products where the underlying source rights permit it.
6. Memberships, courses, or B2B briefings come only after a lane demonstrates repeat demand.
