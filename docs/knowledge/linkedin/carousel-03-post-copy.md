## 投稿本文（そのまま貼る）

```
"We translated the site into Japanese and nothing happened."

I hear this more than any other sentence about Japan. The usual diagnosis
is that the translation wasn't good enough, or that the market wasn't ready.

It is neither. There are two passes, and your page is built for the second.

Western landing pages are engineered for an evaluation pass: is this good,
is this worth it, is this better than the alternative. Every conversion
tool you own operates there.

Japanese consumers tend to run a risk-elimination pass first. Not "is this
good" but "is there a reason not to." It is fast, largely unconscious, and
it happens before any of your persuasion is read.

A single unresolved item ends the visit. Which means your conversion work
is not underperforming — it is never being executed. That is exactly why
adding more benefit copy changes nothing.

One item from that first pass, which surprises almost everyone:

Japan has no cooling-off period for online sales. Most overseas sellers
hear that and relax. But if your store does not clearly display a return
policy, the law supplies one — the buyer may return within 8 days of
receipt, paying return shipping. The absence of a policy does not mean no
returns. It means the default applies, and it is not yours.

The 12 pages below cover all five checks, a twelve-question audit you can
run on your own store this afternoon, and the order to fix them in — the
two cheapest items are the two that gate everything else.

Run the audit. Tell me which question you failed, and I'll tell you what
it is costing.
```

## 内容の要点

- 中核主張: 日本の消費者は評価パスの前にリスク消去パスを走らせる。CROは第2パス専用の道具であり、第1パスで落ちていれば一度も実行されない
- 6ページ目に返品8日デフォルトを配置。最も驚かれる事実であり保存動機になる
- 8ページ目の再定義: 「日本人が慎重なのではなく、曖昧さを誰が引き受けるかのデフォルトが違う」。売り手が抱えるべきリスクを買い手に渡している構図として読まれている
- 9ページ目の12問監査が保存の主動力。読者が自社サイトで即実行できる
- 10ページ目: 最も安い2項目が最も多くをブロックする。決済統合から始めて安い項目を落としたまま公開する典型を指摘

## 公開前の確認（重要）

**返品8日デフォルトの記述は現行法で必ず確認すること。** 特定商取引法上、通信販売は訪問販売等のクーリング・オフの対象外である一方、返品特約の表示がない場合の法定ルールが適用される。デッキ本文は条番号・数値の断定を避け「a return policy is not displayed → the law supplies one → 8 days, buyer pays shipping」の水準に留めてある。編集する場合、断定を増やす方向に書き換えない。
