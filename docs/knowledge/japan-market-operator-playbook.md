# Japan Market Operator — Commercial Playbook

## Positioning

Paradigm acts as an **external Japan market operator**, not a stock-buying distributor. The initial promise is a fast, paid decision on whether and how to enter Japan. Execution expands only after the validation evidence supports it.

## Commercial package

| Phase | Price | Timing | Deliverables | Decision gate |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Paid Market Validation | $5,000 | 1–2 weeks | Demand and competitor view, regulatory screen, channel choice, localized positioning, pricing and unit-economics model, 90-day plan | Go, revise or stop |
| Japan Launch | $20,000 total | 4–8 weeks | Everything above plus localization, Shopify/Amazon launch path, 3PL/returns/customer-support design, launch assets and partner coordination | Brand approves launch and inventory/ad commitments |
| Hybrid Growth | $2,500/month + 10% | 12 months | Japan operations, weekly English reporting, channel/partner management, customer insight and growth execution | Quarterly KPI review |

The $5,000 validation fee is fully credited toward the $20,000 launch total when the brand proceeds within 30 days. External legal advice, laboratory tests, certification fees, inventory, freight, duties, media spend and third-party software are excluded unless a SOW explicitly includes them.

This is the **distribution/operator lane**. Do not quote it interchangeably with the separate $15,000 Country Partner Setup, which is a fixed setup lane without distributor appointment, KPI-linked exclusivity or the 10% revenue share. See [Japan Market Operator — Operating Manual v1](japan-market-operator-operating-manual.md) for routing, stage gates, RACI, finance close, incident handling and Wave 1 execution.

## Ideal customer profile

- Overseas D2C or consumer brand with roughly $2M–$30M annual revenue.
- Gross margin of at least 65%, average order value of $70–$300 and capacity for $10,000+ monthly launch media.
- Visible product-market proof: reviews, repeat purchase, creator content or active paid acquisition.
- No established Japan entity, exclusive agent or mature Japanese sales operation.
- Initial priority: home/storage/workspace, non-electrical wellness, travel, non-food pet, outdoor/hobby and premium design lifestyle.
- Initial exclusions or validation-only: supplements, food, medical claims, cosmetics, electrical/wireless products and child-safety products.

## Outreach sequence

1. Reconfirm the evidence URL, contact route, product constraints and absence of an incumbent exclusive Japan partner.
2. Generate a three-page Japan Opportunity Memo: opportunity thesis, comparable pricing/channels and a 90-day launch path.
3. Send a short permission-first message. Do not lead with a meeting request, exclusivity or the full paid offer.
4. After a positive reply, send the memo plus a localized landing-page mock, short product video concept, simple P&L and proposed validation SOW.
5. Hold a 30-minute qualification call covering revenue, gross margin, inventory, ad budget, decision authority, regulatory history and Japan rights.
6. Issue MSA + Paid Market Validation SOW through Docuseal. Issue the launch SOW and exclusivity addendum only after the validation gate.

## Contract structure

Use three documents:

1. **MSA** — confidentiality, intellectual property, approvals, warranties, liability, data handling, payment, dispute resolution and general termination.
2. **SOW** — phase, deliverables, schedule, client dependencies, acceptance, fees, third-party costs and change control.
3. **Japan Exclusivity Addendum** — territory, specified SKUs, channels, cross-border ecommerce treatment, carve-outs, term and renewal, minimum inventory and ad commitments, sales KPIs, reporting, cure period, conversion to non-exclusive, sell-off and transition.

Define **Net Collected Japan Sales** as cash actually received from Japan-attributed sales, less consumption tax, refunds, chargebacks, discounts, duties paid by the seller and marketplace/payment fees stated in the SOW. Do not calculate the revenue share on uncollected orders.

Initial exclusivity should be 12 months and conditional. It activates only for named SKUs and channels, remains subject to quarterly milestones and converts to non-exclusive after an agreed cure period if either side misses material commitments. Product liability, recalls, importer-of-record responsibility, required insurance and regulatory representations must be allocated explicitly and reviewed by qualified counsel.

## RevenueOS operating stages

`prospect_intake → evidence_verified → memo_ready → human_approved → permission_sent → replied → qualification → validation_sow → paid_validation → launch_sow → operator_contract → active_operator`

No outbound message may move from `memo_ready` to `permission_sent` without human approval and a verified delivery route. Docuseal signed events remain the contract source for customer handoff.
