---
name: Editorial chapter system
---

Use the brief's brand colors and font. Typography and explicit authored points carry
the narrative. No fabricated metrics, simulated product UI, stock claims, or decorative
charts. Chapter label establishes continuity; headline states one idea, points unpack
that idea in sequence, and body supplies a short supporting sentence. Hard cuts follow
the authored audio slots. All motion must be finite and seekable. Keep captions in
the lower safe region and primary copy above it. This template is an explainer lane,
not a replacement for generated cinematic footage or avatar performance.
